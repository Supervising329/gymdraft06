How training works

Start with a conversation, not a checkout.

Tory and John review each inquiry before discussing scheduling, training structure, and cost privately.

The process

Five clear steps from interest to training.

There is no public package selection or automatic purchase. The process begins with fit and clarity.

  1. 01Send your request

    Share your goal, experience, training focus, and general availability.

  2. 02Goal review

    Tory or John reviews the information and responds by email.

  3. 03Personal recommendation

    Discuss the most useful in-person training direction for your goal.

  4. 04Private details

    Review scheduling, training frequency, and cost directly.

  5. 05Begin training

    Move forward after both sides agree that the arrangement is a good fit.

Why pricing is discussed privately

The recommendation comes before the cost.

A useful training plan depends on the client's objective, experience, availability, and required level of support.

Instead of presenting packages that may not fit, Tory and John first learn what the client needs. Scheduling and pricing are then discussed clearly before anyone commits.

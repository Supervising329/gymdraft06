My Strength Training Testimony

Sometimes, life gives us a wake-up call when we least expect it.

For me, that wake-up call came during my annual doctor’s appointment. I noticed that some of my numbers were gradually increasing, and I realized I could no longer take my health for granted. At the same time, I had been dealing with an ongoing neck problem. I kept getting massages, hoping they would resolve the issue, but the relief never lasted.

That experience helped me understand that I needed to do more than temporarily manage the problem—I needed to take action and become stronger.

This realization motivated me to begin strength training again. A significant part of my progress, however, has been having the right person in my corner: my strength coach, Tory!

Tory has played an important role in my journey. She has challenged and encouraged me while helping me recognize that I was capable of more than I sometimes believed. When I needed to be pushed, she pushed me; when things felt difficult, she supported me. Through her guidance and accountability, I learned to keep showing up, even on the days when I didn’t feel motivated.

Getting started was not easy. Many times, making excuses or putting things off would have been more comfortable. Still, with Tory’s encouragement, I continued moving forward, and over time, I began to see meaningful changes.

I became physically stronger, but the transformation extended far beyond my physical fitness. My confidence grew, my energy increased, and my mindset became stronger. I also developed greater discipline and consistency—qualities that have positively affected every area of my life.

Tory helped me understand that strength training is about more than lifting weights. It is about discovering what I am capable of, challenging myself to grow, and refusing to give up when something feels difficult. Each workout became a reminder that I was investing in my health, my future, and myself.

Looking back, I am grateful for that doctor’s appointment because it encouraged me to stop ignoring the signs and start taking action. I am especially grateful that Tory was there to guide and support me throughout the process.

My journey is not about being perfect; it is about being consistent, continuing to show up, and refusing to give up on myself. Tory has been a tremendous part of that journey, and I am thankful for the strength, confidence, and discipline she has helped me build—not only in the gym, but also in my everyday life.

- Debbie

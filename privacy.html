Privacy policy

Last updated August 9, 2026. This draft should be reviewed for the final business name, location, and operating practices before launch.

Information collected

When you submit the consultation form, the business may receive your name, email, optional phone number, fitness goal, training focus, experience level, availability, and message. Do not submit medical records or other highly sensitive information through this website.

How information is used

Information is used to review an inquiry, respond by email, discuss potential training services, maintain necessary business records, and protect the website from misuse.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed by Formspree. Website traffic and performance information may be processed by Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights. These providers operate under their own privacy terms.

Analytics

Analytics may collect aggregated page-view, device, browser, referrer, and approximate location information. Form-field values are not intentionally sent to website analytics.

Retention and security

Inquiry information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your inquiry information by emailing torycurtismassage@comcast.net.
